Listing with George

Priced right, prepped right, sold right

The first conversation is about the number. I pull the sales that actually compare to your home — same area, same size, same condition — and we set a price built to sell, not to sit. You'll see every comp I used.

Then we get the house ready. I'll tell you honestly which fixes pay for themselves and which ones don't, and if you need a painter, a handyman or a landscaper, my Resources You Can Use page is full of people I trust with my own name.

Once we're live, your home goes on the MLS with professional marketing through CENTURY 21, and I handle every showing, every offer and every negotiation — in English or Spanish, so nothing gets lost between you and the buyer.
