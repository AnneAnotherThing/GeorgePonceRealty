The rental path

Renting with me is step one, not a dead end.

We find you a place you can afford this month. Then, while you live there, we build the credit, the savings and the paperwork that turn a renter into a homeowner. Most of my clients get there in one to two years.

How it works

A plan, not a promise

Plenty of people will tell you "rent now, buy later." The difference here is that we write the plan down. From the first month you'll know exactly what we're working on, what it costs, and when we check in next.

You don't need perfect credit, a big down payment, or a green light from anyone else to start. You need a place to live and the willingness to take a few small steps on a schedule. I handle the rest with you.

And if the answer to "can I buy yet?" is "not yet", I'll tell you straight, along with exactly what has to change and how long it usually takes.

Credit & qualification

Credit is a tool, not a judgment

Your score is just a number that tells lenders how you've handled bills lately. Numbers move. Here's what we work with.

What your score actually means

Most lenders want to see somewhere around 580 to 640 before they'll talk seriously, and better rates start around 700. If you're below that today, you're not out. You're early.

  • 700+: strong options and the best rates
  • 640–699: solid, most loan programs open up
  • 580–639: workable, often FHA territory
  • Below 580: we build for 6 to 12 months first

How we build it together

At the month-three check-in we read your report side by side and pick the two or three moves that matter most. Usually it's some mix of:

  • Fixing errors on the report (more common than you'd think)
  • Paying small collections strategically, not all at once
  • Getting card balances under 30% of the limit
  • Adding rent reporting so the rent you already pay counts

What the timeline usually looks like

Everyone asks. Honest answer: it depends where you start, but most people see real movement in 6 months and are lender-ready in 12 to 24. The plan is built around your report, not a template.

The one thing that breaks a timeline is new debt. Big purchases wait until after closing. I'll remind you.

Common questions

Things renters actually ask me

Do I need good credit just to rent with you?

No. Landlords have their own requirements and I know which ones are flexible. Part of my job is matching your situation today to a landlord who'll say yes. The credit work is for the house later, not the rental now.

How much do you charge me for all this?

Nothing out of your pocket. On rentals and purchases I'm paid through the transaction, the same way any agent is. The credit coaching and check-ins are part of how I work, not a service you buy.

What if my income is cash or I have more than one job?

Very normal in my business. There are lenders who work well with self-employment and multiple income streams; it mostly comes down to documenting it right, and we start that paper trail early so it's ready when you are.

Can we do everything in Spanish?

Yes. Every conversation, every document walkthrough, every negotiation. You should never sign something you don't fully understand, in either language.

What if I'm not sure I ever want to buy?

Then we find you a great rental and that's that. No pressure, ever. Some clients rent with me for years before the buying conversation starts, and some never have it. The door just stays open.

I had an eviction / broken lease / bankruptcy. Is that the end?

No, but it changes the order of operations. Evictions and bankruptcies age off, and lenders care how long ago and what's happened since. We'll be honest about the wait and use the time to build everything else.

Do you only work in certain neighborhoods?

I work across the Phoenix valley: Phoenix, Glendale, Maryvale, Mesa, Avondale, Tolleson, Laveen and the West Valley. If you have a school district in mind, start there and I'll tell you what's realistic.

What happens after I fill out the form?

I call or text you, usually the same day. We talk for fifteen or twenty minutes, I ask the rest of my questions, and you leave the call knowing your next two steps. That's it. No contract, no obligation.

Ready to find your place?

Five questions, about two minutes. I'll take it from there, in English or Spanish.
