First question

What can I actually afford?

This is where every serious conversation starts. The estimate here uses the same guideline lenders use: your housing payment should stay near 28% of your monthly income, and all your debt payments together under about 36%.

It's a starting point, not a pre-approval. Rates, programs and down-payment help can move the number in your favor, and that's exactly what we go over on a call.

The journey

From first call to flag day

Six stages. Some take a week, some take a month. You'll always know which one we're in and what comes next.

01
Stage 1 · Week 1
Pre-approval
You meet a lender I trust and they tell us the real budget. This costs nothing and locks nothing in. It just means sellers take your offer seriously.
02
Stage 2 · Weeks 1–6
The search
We tour homes inside the budget, in the areas you named. I'll point out the problems, not just the granite countertops. You never get talked into anything.
03
Stage 3 · A few days
The offer
We look at what similar homes actually sold for, decide the number together, and I negotiate for you. Sometimes we win, sometimes we walk. Walking is allowed.
04
Stage 4 · Weeks 1–2 under contract
Inspection & appraisal
A licensed inspector goes through everything and we read the report together. If something big turns up, we renegotiate or we leave with your earnest money protected.
05

Closing
Final walkthrough, then signatures at the title company. I sit next to you the whole time, and nothing gets signed that we haven't already gone over in your language.

I bring the flag to every closing. Before I leave, we find the right place to hang it. The house is yours, and so is the flag. That part isn't a marketing line.

Straight talk

Do you really need a realtor?

Honest answer: you can buy a home without one. People do. Here's what I actually do all day, so you can decide with your eyes open.

“I'll save money going alone.”

The seller's side almost always has a professional negotiating for them from day one. Walking in alone doesn't make the deal cheaper — it makes the table uneven. And before you sign anything with me, we go over exactly what my help costs and how it gets paid. No surprises, in either language.

“The listing agent can help me too.”

The listing agent's job is to get the seller the best possible deal. That's the whole job. You deserve someone whose only job is you — especially in the biggest purchase of your life.

“The internet already told me what it's worth.”

An algorithm has never walked the street, smelled the carpet, or looked at the roof. Real value comes from comparable sales — and reading those right is half of what you hire me for.

“Agents just unlock doors.”

The door is the easy part. Inspections, appraisal gaps, repair negotiations, title, deadlines that can cost you your earnest money — that's the actual work, and it's where deals are saved or lost.
