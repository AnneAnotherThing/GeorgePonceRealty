Investing with George

Real numbers before real offers

Every investment conversation starts the same way a homebuying one does: with the honest math. What the home would rent for, what the payment really costs with taxes and insurance, what's left over, and what surprises to budget for. If the numbers don't work, I'll say so.

Many of my investors started as my renters and buyers. They know the Phoenix valley rents because they've paid them, and that experience is an advantage — you know exactly what a good rental feels like from the other side of the lease.

And you won't do it alone. Lenders who work with investors, inspectors, and the tradespeople who keep a rental running are all on my Resources You Can Use page — the same people I'd call for my own properties.
